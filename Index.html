    Community rules

    Glimpse is a wall everyone shares. Help keep it a place people enjoy visiting.

    1. Be kind. No harassment, bullying, threats, or hate toward anyone or any group.
    2. Keep it suitable for everyone. No nudity, sexual content, or graphic violence.
    3. Nothing illegal or dangerous, and nothing that encourages people to hurt themselves or others.
    4. Respect privacy. Don't share other people's personal details, or photos and videos of people who wouldn't want them posted.
    5. Share what's yours to share. Only post photos, videos and words you have the right to post.
    6. No spam, scams or ads.
    7. Time capsules follow the same rules. Moderators can open a sealed capsule if it's reported.

    Posts that break these rules are removed, and people who keep breaking them can't post any more. If you see something wrong, open it and tap Report. To stop seeing someone's posts, tap Block.
