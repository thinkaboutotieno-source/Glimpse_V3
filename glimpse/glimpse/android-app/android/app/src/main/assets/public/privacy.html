Privacy policy

Glimpse · last updated [date]

Glimpse is a shared wall of photos, videos, quotes and thoughts, run by [your name or organisation]. This page explains what we collect and why. Questions or requests: [contact email].

What you post

Everything you post (photos, videos, quotes, thoughts, captions and the name you type) is shown publicly to everyone who visits the wall. You don't need to give your real name. Time capsules stay hidden until their opening date, except that moderators can view one if it's reported.

Voice notes, replies and private walls

  • Voice notes you record or upload are stored and played publicly like any other post. Glimpse only uses your microphone while you're recording, after you tap the record button.
  • Replies to thoughts are public, like posts.
  • Private walls aren't listed anywhere, but anyone who has a wall's link or QR code can see and add to it. Don't treat a private wall as secret. Whoever created a wall can remove posts and replies on it.

Where a post comes from

Unless you untick it when posting, a post shows the city of your device's time zone (for example "Nairobi, Africa") so it can appear on the Constellations map. We never use GPS or your exact location, and we don't keep the time zone itself, just that city name.

The Void

When you delete one of your posts, it's kept in The Void for 30 days so you can restore it, then erased for good (including its photo, video or recording). You can erase it sooner from The Void. Only you can see your Void.

Channels, portals and comets

  • Channels are public and listed: anyone can see posts in them. Adding a #channel name to a public post also shows it in that channel.
  • Portals link two walls chosen by their hosts. Posts on one appear on the other.
  • Comets (messages in a bottle) go to one random person who's online, along with the city of your time zone unless you've turned that off. We keep a comet for 7 days so its reply can find its way back to you, then delete it. The person who receives it never learns who you are.

Optional features that talk to other services

  • Live weather (off until you turn it on in the theme menu) asks the free Open-Meteo service for the weather at your time zone's city. Only that city's approximate coordinates are sent.
  • Translate first tries your browser's own on-device translator. If it can't, the text of that post is sent to [your translation service, if you set one up] to be translated.
  • Sky view uses your phone's motion sensors and, only if you tap "Camera", its camera. Nothing from the camera or sensors is uploaded.

Friends

  • Your device gets a friend code. If you choose to add friends, we store the name you set, your friend list and pending friend requests, linked to your anonymous device key.
  • Friends-only posts are only shown to you and the people on your friend list. They never appear in public lists or link previews. Moderators can still review them if they're reported.
  • Anyone with your invite link can add you as a friend. You can replace the link at any time.

What we store automatically

  • An anonymous device ID. Your browser or the app creates a random ID. We store a scrambled (hashed) version with your posts so that people can block you and we can stop someone who breaks the rules. It isn't linked to your name, phone number or account.
  • A scrambled version of your IP address with each post and report, for the same reason, and briefly in memory to limit how fast anyone can post.
  • Reports you send: the reason, any note you add, and when.
  • Live presence. While the site is open, the server counts how many people are on the same wall (shown as "here now" and as drifting lights). It uses a random code for each open page and keeps nothing once you leave.

We don't use advertising, analytics or tracking cookies, and we don't sell or share your data.

Graffiti, badges and the Story of the Day

  • Graffiti hour strokes are shared live with everyone on the wall and saved as a public mural when the hour ends.
  • Badges you earn are saved with your friend code so your friends can see them.
  • The Story of the Day video is made in your browser from posts already on the wall. It's never uploaded unless you share it yourself.
  • "After dark only" posts are stored like any other post; each viewer's own device decides when it's night.

Your theme song

Music you pick as your theme song is stored only in your browser on your device. It's never uploaded, and nobody else can hear it.

On your device

Your device remembers your chosen theme, sound and calm-mode settings, the name you last used, posts and replies you liked or reported, people you blocked, walls you visited, and private keys that let you delete your own posts and host your walls. These stay on your device.

How long we keep things

Posts stay until you delete them (then 30 days in The Void), or until a moderator removes them for breaking the community rules. Deleting a post removes its photo or video file from our server.

Your choices

  • Delete any post you made from this device: open it and tap Delete.
  • To remove posts you can no longer delete yourself, or for any other request, email [contact email].
